Brand Identity + Marketing

MikiZaah LTD

Designed a modern and professional brand identity for a growing business, including logo, business cards, and social media assets.

Client

MikiZaah LTD

Services

Logo · Flyers · Social Ads

Year

2025

Tools

Photoshop · Illustrator

01 — The Problem

AI designs and poor branding.

MikiZaah LTD was operating with a logo and marketing materials that felt AI-generated and inconsistent across platforms. The brand needed to look as credible as the service it delivered — especially when pitching to new clients.

The challenge: modernize the identity without losing recognition, and build a marketing system that could be reused across flyers, social media, and print.

02 — The Approach

Rebuild the foundation, then extend the system.

Research & Positioning

Studied competitors in the local laundry and waste picking industry and identified a visual gap: most brands looked generic. I positioned MikiZaah LTD to feel modern, confident, and distinct.

Logo Redesign

Developed a clean, geometric mark with strong balance and scalability — readable at app-icon size and on large signage.

Visual System

Defined a color palette, typography hierarchy, and layout rules so every future asset stays on-brand.

Marketing Kit

Applied the system to flyers, social ads, and promo banners — giving the brand a coherent presence across all touchpoints.

03 — The Deliverables

What was delivered.

A complete brand system, ready to use across print and digital.

04 — The Result

A brand that finally looks as strong as it performs.

"The client reported a stronger first impression during client pitches and adopted the new system across all marketing materials."
