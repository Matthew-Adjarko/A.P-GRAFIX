01 — The Problem

AI-generated identity that didn't match the ambition.

Great SB Ushering & Event Agency was operating with a flyer and marketing materials that felt unprofessional and inconsistent across platforms. The brand needed to look as credible as the service it delivered — especially when pitching to new clients. and inconsistent across platforms. The brand needed to look as credible as the service it delivered — especially when pitching to new clients.

The challenge: modernize the identity without losing recognition, and build a marketing system that could be reused across flyers, social media, and print.

02 — The Approach

Rebuild the foundation, then extend the system.

Research & Positioning

Studied competitors in the ushering and event planning industry and identified a visual gap: most brands looked generic. I positioned Great SB Ushering & Event Agency to feel modern, confident, and distinct.

Social Media Assets

Designed a clean, geometric mark with strong balance and scalability — readable at app-icon size and on large signage.

Visual System

Defined a color palette, typography hierarchy, and layout rules so every future asset stays on-brand.

Marketing Kit

Applied the system to flyers, and introductory social ads giving the brand a coherent presence across all touch points.

03 — The Deliverables

What was delivered.

A new flyer and marketing materials for Great SB Ushering & Event Agency.

04 — The Result

A brand that finally looks as strong as it performs.

The client reported a stronger first impression during client pitches and adopted the new system across all marketing materials.
