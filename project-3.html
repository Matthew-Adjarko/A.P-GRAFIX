01 — The Problem

A brand without social media marketing designs to pitch new clients.

Ever Bags & Accessories was operating without social media marketing materials. The brand needed to look as credible as the service it delivered — especially when new clients see it.

The challenge: modernize the identity without losing recognition, and build a marketing system that could be reused.

02 — The Approach

Rebuild the foundation, then extend the system.

Research & Positioning

Studied how emotional and relatable design can win clients. I positioned Ever Bags & Accessories to feel modern, confident, and distinct.

Social Media Assets

Developed a clean, social media-friendly design that resonates with the target audience.

Visual System

Defined a color palette, typography hierarchy, and layout rules so every future asset stays on-brand.

Marketing Kit

Applied the system to flyers, social ads, and delivery cards giving the brand a coherent presence across all touchpoints.

03 — The Deliverables

What was delivered.

A complete brand system, ready to use across print and digital.

04 — The Result

A brand that finally looks as strong as it performs.

The client reported a stronger engagement on social media and adopted the new system across all marketing materials.
